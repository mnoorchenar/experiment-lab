Sequential Testing & the Peeking Problem

When would you use this? Every time you check an ongoing A/B test before it is finished, you risk seeing a spurious "significant" result by chance — the peeking problem. Sequential testing methods (like O'Brien-Fleming) let you monitor experiments early while controlling the false positive rate. This simulation shows exactly why peeking is dangerous and how sequential testing corrects it.
