📈 Sequential Testing & the Peeking Problem

When would you use this? Every time you check an ongoing A/B test before it is finished, you risk seeing a spurious "significant" result just by chance. This is called the peeking problem. Sequential testing methods — like O'Brien-Fleming — let you check results early while keeping the false positive rate under control. Use this simulation to see exactly why peeking is dangerous and how sequential testing fixes it.
