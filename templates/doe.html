Two-Factor Experiment Analyzer (DoE)

When would you use this? When testing two decisions simultaneously — e.g., email subject line AND send time, or button color AND page layout. A factorial design is more efficient than two separate A/B tests and uniquely reveals whether the two factors interact with each other (i.e., whether the best subject line depends on the send time).

Experiment Setup
Enter the observed value (or comma-separated list of replicate observations) for each factor-level combination. Rows = Factor A levels, Columns = Factor B levels.

Interaction Plot
