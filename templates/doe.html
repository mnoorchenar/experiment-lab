🔬 Two-Factor Experiment Analyzer (DoE)

When would you use this? When you are testing two decisions at the same time — e.g., email subject line AND send time, or button color AND page layout. Running them together in a factorial design is more efficient than two separate A/B tests and uniquely reveals whether the two factors interact with each other (i.e., whether the best subject line depends on the send time).
